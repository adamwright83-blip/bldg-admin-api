Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

JOYSTICK is operated by Joystick Inc. ("JOYSTICK", "we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use gojoystick.com and the JOYSTICK app, why, who else handles it, and the choices you have. Questions: [privacy contact email].

What we collect

When you sign up or log in with Google

Your name, email address and Google account ID. That is all signing in shares.

When you connect Google (only if you choose to)

With your permission, JOYSTICK gets read-only access to your Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive. It reads your sent email and the messages it needs to understand your work, your calendar events, and the documents you own or work on. It can't change, delete or send anything with this access. If you later choose to send an email from JOYSTICK, it asks Google separately for permission to send, and it sends only a message you have approved word for word.

If you connect Slack

Read access to the conversations you can see, to understand the people and work involved.

Public information

To understand your business, JOYSTICK may look up public information about it and you: public business listings (for example Google Maps), your business's website, public profile pages that search engines index, and public posts on an X (Twitter) account confirmed to be yours. It never logs in to or opens a private page on LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook or any other site.

What you tell us

Your answers and corrections, your business details, and billing details. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.

How you use JOYSTICK

Product analytics about which screens you use and when, so we can improve JOYSTICK. The contents of your email, calendar, documents and conversations are never sent to analytics.

How we use it

  • To build JOYSTICK's understanding of your business (your customers, the work in motion, what has gone quiet) and show it back to you, in the game and in your workspace.
  • To prepare messages and suggestions for you to review. Nothing is sent without your approval.
  • To run your account, provide support, keep JOYSTICK secure, and bill your subscription.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google user data

JOYSTICK's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the features you see in JOYSTICK.
  • We do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide those features (the service providers below), to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.
  • We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • No person at JOYSTICK reads your Google data unless you ask us to (for support), it's needed for security or to comply with law, or it has been aggregated and anonymized.

Who else handles your data

We use service providers who process data on our behalf, under contract, only to run JOYSTICK:

  • AI services: Google (Gemini) and Anthropic (Claude) receive short excerpts of what JOYSTICK reads, to understand it. Under their terms for business use of their APIs, they do not use this data to train their models. xAI receives only requests about public X posts.
  • Infrastructure: Railway (servers and database) and Vercel (website delivery).
  • Payments: Stripe.
  • Analytics: PostHog, without the contents of your data.
  • Google Maps Platform: to look up public business listings.

How we protect it

  • Access to Google and Slack is read-only unless you separately grant permission to send.
  • The access keys those services give us are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM).
  • Bank, card and identity numbers, and passwords or keys, are removed from the email and documents JOYSTICK reads before anything is stored or sent to an AI service. Documents such as bank statements, tax returns and medical records are skipped entirely.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google or Slack from JOYSTICK at any time; we delete our copy of the access keys. You can also remove JOYSTICK's access in your Google Account.
  • Correct anything JOYSTICK understood about you; your correction always wins over what it inferred.
  • Delete your account and the data JOYSTICK keeps about you by emailing [privacy contact email]. We delete it within 30 days, except what we must keep by law (such as billing records).
  • Access a copy of the information we hold about you, by email.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete what we hold about you, except records we must keep by law.

Children

JOYSTICK is for people running a business and is not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app or by email before the change applies.
