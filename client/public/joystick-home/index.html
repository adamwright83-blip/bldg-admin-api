A BUSINESS YOU CAN PLAY.

A little company.
A different way forward.

You’re good at what you do. Joystick brings a little adventure to the rest.

It finds opportunities in your customer history and business records, then builds games around taking the next step. Help a neighbor. Open a door. Find your way back to someone you’ve been meaning to reach.

You review the real messages and choose the real actions. The companions make getting there more interesting.
