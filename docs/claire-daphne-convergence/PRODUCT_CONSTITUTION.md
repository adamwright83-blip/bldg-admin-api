# JOYSTICK product constitution and operating contract

Owner request: 2026-10-12. Engineering assignment: GitHub issue #565. This contract records the creator's intended product and the boundaries already established by the architecture, Dialogue Atlas, Weekly Mission Readiness, Daphne architecture, and JOYSTICK system map. Current production behavior is evidence of implementation, not a substitute for intended behavior.

## Purpose

JOYSTICK is a playable operating system for running a real business. Verified business needs become compelling playable work that produces real actions, acquired customers, paid orders, and revenue. A created mission, successful game interaction, or passing test does not prove a business outcome.

Claire is the operator's proactive strategist and execution partner. She understands performance, identifies bottlenecks, plans feasible weekly and daily work, helps execute, and follows results. Her voice is direct, dry, observant, disciplined, quietly competitive, and capable of earned warmth. She acknowledges corrections, states uncertainty, and gives a useful next action.

Daphne learns from explicit preferences, corrections, interactions, and verified outcomes. She stores testable hypotheses rather than diagnoses: skipped cold calls can reflect timing, burden, insufficient immersion, or other causes. Claire and Daphne may select authorized parameters of approved experiences. Mitch owns authored gameplay and fictional experiences; Adam retains creative authority.

## End-to-end contract

1. Read tenant- and operator-scoped evidence, retaining freshness, coverage, provenance, period and explicit missing data. Unknown is not zero. Costs unavailable means profit unavailable.
2. Explain a sourced opportunity and its uncertainty. Build feasible strategy using the operator's actual goal, commitments, capacity and readiness.
3. Preserve the weekly agreement and explain daily overrides. Obtain the applicable approval through existing authority. Keep one work identity through call, Day Line, Admin, Driver, game and debrief.
4. Verify every write with its owning system and canonical readback: correct actor, business date, item identities and count. Speech must distinguish proposal, acceptance, scheduling, action and outcome. Retries and interrupted calls must not duplicate work.
5. Record execution through the owner domain. Keep operator testimony, call/visit, completion, conversion, collected payment and fictional consequence distinct.
6. Feed linked evidence into the next Claire recommendation and Daphne learning. Select only registered Mitch-approved presentations with fresh operator consent. Measure actual exposure, initiation, completion and downstream business outcomes separately. Preserve the business objective during a presentation experiment.
7. Autonomy is earned, consented, bounded and reversible. Readiness evaluation never grants authority. Source degradation, defects or revocation reduce eligibility immediately.

## Required acceptance

- Invitation and callback survive process restart with the correct planning purpose and actor. Multi-turn correction, interruption, genuine goodbye and unfinished work sentences route accurately.
- Remaining-week planning uses real goals and commitments, accepts explicit approval, and has durable per-day receipts. Partial failure and delayed operations are spoken truthfully.
- Revenue, orders, AOV, comparisons, customer metrics and composable derived metrics use canonical source adapters, currency/unit/period checks and governed calculations. Missing inputs are individually reported, never silently dropped or substituted.
- Proactive Laundry Butler evidence produces a specific bottleneck hypothesis, an approved feasible sales mission or non-field marketing challenge, a verified Day Line item and an independently verified outcome.
- A presentation experiment uses the same real objective, explicit scoped consent, approved variants, durable assignment and observed outcome; timing/location reasons and revoked consent suppress unsupported immersion claims.
- A production-shaped browser/API/database journey survives restart and action replay. Final production certification additionally requires the exact deployed SHA/flags and real operator call evidence.
