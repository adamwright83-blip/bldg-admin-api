# ONE PLACE, NOT THREE SCREENS

They live in **one continuously navigable ruin**.

If Lean is a home screen, Wire is a UI overlay, and Shutter is a mission card, we have built three widgets that share a skin. Widgets are what you complete. Places are what you return to.

**Continuous:**
- Coast + Stronghold as one framed space you can lean through.
- Earned gold seams exist *in* that space as drawable wires.
- The dusk shutter is a specific aperture *in the Stronghold wall of that same space*.
- Time-of-day light is global.

**May become a set-piece later (not Act I graybox):**
- M02’s surgical table can *push in* from the same space (camera dollies to a bench that was always in frame) then dollies back. It is not a different app screen.
- After No’s hallway-echo is the same seam seen from a few meters left — lean finds it.

If you cannot see Monday’s gold *while* rattling Friday’s shutter, it is not a place.

# 1. LEAN

### Feel test
**Fails if it feels like scrolling a hero image.**
Passes if your wrist starts hunting a sliver of geometry the default frame hid, and you forget you opened a product.

### Composition (portrait)
Two-thirds coast ruin, gold inlay nearer than the hill. Stronghold block left or right, one dark window. Horizon high. One dead or gold seam crossing stone. No chrome. No hamburger.

### Thumb
One-finger drag anywhere on stone/sky — not on a handle.
The world has mass. Finger moves 1, layers move 0.3 / 0.6 / 1.1. Release: ease-out, short coast, settle. Flick dies in half a second. No rubber-band bounce like Safari.

### What moves immediately
Near gold wire shears against far hill. Stronghold window slides off the sea. A colonnade edge enters from the side only at extreme lean.

### Camera
No free orbit. No pitch into dirt. A constrained gimbal: yaw + tiny parallax. You are a person leaning on a parapet, not a drone.

### Sound
Cloth/gravel under drag if you want one layer. No whoosh. No UI tick. Silent is legal.

### Reads
Device clock (sun angle, wet/dry if we even do weather). Which seams exist and whether gold. Whether Stronghold night-occupied (post-keep).

### Forbidden
Lean does not light seams. Does not arm missions. Does not spawn courtyard *content* — only reveals authored geometry that was always modeled.

### First time
Default frame is slightly *wrong* — seam not centered — so the first drag feels like correcting a stance.

### Twentieth time
Same mass. Different sun. You go to *your* angle (the one that puts gold on the colonnade edge). Nothing new unlocked. You are visiting.

### Farming
There is no score. Extreme lean does not reveal a treat on visit 20 that visit 1 hid behind a gate. If they drag for dopamine, they get inertia. Boredom is the cap.

### No gold yet
You can still lean. Dead seam is in the nearer plane and looks like a cut that never filled. Place exists before progress.

### After success
Gold wire is simply in the near plane. Night-keep: interior light spills from Stronghold, visible when you lean toward it.

### After miss
Nothing about lean changes except the shutter state you can frame.

### Frames
**F1** Default: seam left-of-center, sea, dark window.  
**GESTURE** Drag right.  
**F2** Gold wire (if earned) shears; colonnade begins.  
**GESTURE** Hold at the stop.  
**F3** Stillness. Sun on wire. No popup.  
**GESTURE** Release.  
**F4** Ease back a few degrees, not home unless they want home.  
**GESTURE** Drag left toward Stronghold.  
**F5** Window frames sea. If night-occupied, warm spill.  
**GESTURE** None.  
**F6** You close the phone or you stay. The toy already did its job.

# 2. WIRE / SLACK PLUMB

### Feel test
**Fails if it feels like drawing on a progress bar.**
Passes if it feels like a stringed instrument that can be out of tune, and you accept that.

### Composition
Same ruin. Seams are physical inlay you can put a finger on. M03 incomplete = a plumb bob hanging a finger’s width off a tick mark, same family of metal.

### Thumb
Contact on the wire (fat hit target, visually thin). Draw *along* the authored stroke only. Off-stroke: scrape, no song. Speed: slow enough and it voices; rush and rasp.

Plumb: grab bob, lift against a little spring, release. It falls and *misses the tick every time* if the file is unfinished.

### Weight
Wire has tension if gold exists. Spring on the bob is cheap and obvious. Finger-up = slack immediately.

### Camera
Does not cut. Slight settle toward the touched seam so you are not fighting Lean. After release, stay.

### Sound
Gold, first trace of the session: one taut note, then mute.  
Rush: dry rasp.  
Slack M03: leather-cord creak, no pitch.  
Second trace same morning: air, almost nothing.  
Never a success arpeggio.

### Reads
Seam gold vs dead vs M03-armed/incomplete vs M03-played-still-no (muted gold / double exposure — bob may rest closer but still not kiss if we want “went, still no” as a dull thud + still-open file).

Important: **thud is not close.** Close would be fake completion. Thud = body passed. Kiss = never from toy.

### Forbidden
Tracing does not complete M03. Lifting the bob does not qualify anything. Sound is not XP.

### First time
Finger finds metal by accident while leaning. Note or rasp surprises.

### Twentieth
You know which seam sings and which only creaks. You touch the slack one because it is a pen click. You do not expect a new note.

### Farming
Mute after one honest trace per seam per local calendar morning. Not a punishment toast. Physics just stopped caring.

### State absent
No second line → no bob. Don’t ghost a tutorial bob.

### After M01 success
That seam is taut. One note.

### After M03 armed, not played
Bob misses forever. Creak.

### After M03 played, still no
Dull thud if they retrace the *path* that was verified — and only if we can read “played.” If we cannot read it safely, keep creak. Never kiss.

### After miss Hold
Wire unchanged.

### Frames
**F1** Lean frame. Gold seam + slack parallel.  
**GESTURE** Finger down on gold.  
**F2** Wire brightens under skin, no outline button.  
**GESTURE** Draw the stroke slow.  
**F3** One note. Finger reaches the end.  
**GESTURE** Lift.  
**F4** Wire looks ordinary again.  
**GESTURE** Grab bob, lift.  
**F5** Bob rises, tick waits below.  
**GESTURE** Release.  
**F6** Miss. Same miss. No text.  
**GESTURE** Draw gold again.  
**F7** Almost silence. Morning already used the note.

# 3. SHUTTER

### Feel test
**Fails if it feels like tapping a locked achievement.**
Passes if it feels like a real window that either gives onto night air or stays stuck in the frame, and your body believes the light.

### Composition
Same ruin. Stronghold wall has a shuttered opening you can lean to. Default morning: shutter closed, real sun on the *outside* wall tracks device time toward a promise if a Hold exists.

### Thumb
On closed shutter after miss or with no keep: rattle. Lateral micro-shake, clack, spring back shut.  
On kept night: vertical lift, weight, it stays up. You can pull it down again; that is play, not un-keeping.

Outside wall: no slider. Light is a read of clock vs promise. You watch.

### Weight
Shutter has mass. Lift needs a committed swipe, not a tap. Rattle is short travel.

### Camera
Lean to the wall. No interior load screen. When open, you see the same sea from inside the thickness of the wall.

### Sound
Rattle: wood/bronze. Lift: pulley, one knock at the top. Wind if open at night. No “unlocked.”

### Reads
Hold promised? Window time? Keep fired? Missed (promise existed, keep did not fire, window passed)? Night-occupied flag.

### Forbidden
Rattle does not extend the window. Lift without keep is impossible — not “locked padlock icon,” just *stuck*. Watching light does not create a Hold.

### First time (no Hold ever)
Shutter is architecture. You can rattle. Stuck. Fine.

### First Hold pending
Exterior light climbs toward the lintel as dusk approaches. You may stand there like an idiot watching a wall. Good.

### Twentieth
You lean to *your* window the way you lean to *your* gold angle. If nothing is pending, it is just a window. Open only if a keep already happened.

### Farming
Opening and closing a kept shutter is allowed and gets boring, like a real sash. No loot behind it.

### After keep
Night, lift works, Stronghold reads occupied. Lean from F5 of Lean sees spill.

### After miss
Stuck + rattle. Exterior wall no longer climbing — the climb was the window. Now just night on a closed face.

### Frames
**F1** Afternoon. Light halfway up the outside wall. Shutter shut.  
**GESTURE** None. Watch.  
**F2** Later open. Light higher. Still shut.  
**GESTURE** Try lift.  
**F3** It doesn’t travel. Not a lock glyph. Hand failed.  
*(cut to after keep, night)*  
**F4** Same wall, interior spill at the cracks.  
**GESTURE** Committed swipe up.  
**F5** Shutter rises. Sea through the thickness.  
**GESTURE** Rattle is gone; you can pull it down.  
**F6** Down. Spill remains in the room. Keep is not undone.

*(miss branch instead of F4)*  
**F4b** Night, no spill.  
**GESTURE** Rattle.  
**F5b** Clack. Shut. Gold seams behind you still lit if earned.

# THE FIRST 15-MINUTE GRAYBOX

Ugly. One scene. Device clock faked with a debug slider if we must — slider is tester-only, not player.

**Minute 0–3**  
Open. Drag the coast. Prove inertia. Prove a colonnade exists only at lean. If they say “wallpaper,” stop. Idea is dead.

**Minute 3–6**  
Put gold on one seam by **debug state**, not by playing a mission. Draw it. One note. Draw again. Mute. If they say “progress bar,” stop.

**Minute 6–9**  
Spawn slack bob. Lift, miss, lift, miss. If they laugh or click-pen it, Wire is real. If they look for a fill meter, dead.

**Minute 9–12**  
Scrub fake clock toward dusk. Light climbs wall. Do not let them “complete” anything. Ask: would you watch this tomorrow for an appointment you actually have?

**Minute 12–15**  
Flip two states only: KEEP (shutter lifts, night spill) vs MISS (rattle). Same camera. No Claire. No copy.

**Pass:** they linger after they understand there is no prize.  
**Fail:** they ask what they unlocked.

If it fails in fifteen minutes, more prose is vanity. If it passes, World Dwell is a layer, and Narrator can stay silent on quiet mornings without the product feeling unfinished.
