# DOCUMENT 2 — GOLDLINE REALITY BRIDGE v1.0

**Audience:** product owner + implementation agent.
**Purpose:** keep the fantasy and the real business useful to one another without making either impersonate the other.

## Permanent law

> **Every system has a Fantasy Contract and a Reality Contract. Neither is allowed to impersonate the other.**

Fantasy must remain fantasy.

Reality must remain true.

---

# 1. Basic bridge rule

A fantasy campaign may eventually reach something the fantasy world cannot resolve internally.

The Gold Line disappears beyond the available world.

At that point, a meaningful real-world action can become part of the adventure.

The real outcome then influences what the fantasy becomes.

No productivity break.

No “go do your work and come back.”

The player is still pursuing the same mission.

---

# 2. What the game may know

Goldline may react only to things it genuinely knows or the player has explicitly reported.

Different forms of evidence prove different things.

Example:

**GPS arrival**

Can support:

> player reached location

Cannot support:

> player pitched somebody

> decision-maker was present

> sale occurred

**Photo**

Can support:

> submitted image depicts whatever is visibly present

Cannot automatically support:

> conversation happened before/after photo

**Message sent**

Can support:

> message left the system

Cannot support:

> recipient read it

> recipient liked it

> recipient replied

**User report**

Can support:

> player reported this outcome

Does not silently become externally verified truth.

---

# 3. Fantasy response principle

Real events should not be mirrored literally unless doing so produces good fiction.

Arrival does not need to cause:

> “ARRIVAL VERIFIED.”

It could cause:

* brazier ignites;
* beacon wakes;
* dormant statue turns;
* rope tightens;
* distant light appears.

A reply may cause:

* dormant Echo flashes;
* bell rings aboard Wayward;
* faraway structure changes;
* previously dead Line returns.

The fantasy expresses state.

It does not expose databases.

# 4. Mara

### Fantasy truth

Mara finds places other people overlook.

She thinks incomplete maps are more honest than finished ones.

### After Mara is free

Goldline can help the player find new real places worth going.

### May

* suggest a real place;
* present public information;
* explain why it may matter;
* show distance/type/known facts;
* prepare a short briefing;
* remember places already investigated;
* distinguish visited from still-unvisited.

### May not

* invent a person;
* invent a role;
* invent an address;
* invent business status;
* claim a conversation happened;
* claim somebody was reached;
* convert uncertain information into fact.

When information is missing:

> “Not on the map yet.”

### Fantasy reactions

Candidate identified
→ Mara adds something to chart wall.

Place physically reached
→ corresponding fantasy landmark can wake.

New information reported
→ Mara can revise her map while preserving source/provenance.

Nobody available
→ location may remain unresolved.

Definitive no
→ some path may die, shift, reveal something else or simply remain closed.

# 5. Sable

### Fantasy truth

Sable cannot forget.

### After Sable is available

Goldline can preserve and retrieve meaningful history from previous real encounters.

### May

* remember what happened before;
* distinguish who was contacted;
* remember prior objections;
* remember promises and follow-up timing;
* surface unresolved information;
* tell player what is known vs merely reported.

### May not

* rewrite history;
* upgrade assertions into verification;
* fill missing details because they would be useful;
* infer a response that never occurred.

### Fantasy expression

Instead of a generic record screen, Sable can interrupt:

> “You've been here before.”

or:

> “That isn't what he said last time.”

Detailed factual UI can still exist where necessary, but Sable remains a character rather than a CRM mascot.

# 11. Unknown remains useful

Goldline must be comfortable with:

* unknown;
* pending;
* no response;
* no decision;
* wrong person;
* closed door;
* rejection.

The fantasy does not need to reward all of them identically.

Sometimes useful information changes geography.

Sometimes an Echo remains.

Sometimes nothing major happens.

The system must not invent progress to protect the player.

---

# 12. Echoes

An unresolved real state may be represented as an Echo.

Thin Gold Line.

Pulse.

Distant tone.

Visible connection that has not returned.

Later, if reality changes, the Echo may resolve while the player is elsewhere.

The world can react dramatically without a dashboard notification.

---

# 13. Existing Greystar / Colosseum bridge

This is already implemented and must not be reinvented from the Bible.

Current production canon on `main` uses:

* six **fictional** Colosseum doors;
* five authoritative real Greystar Koreatown targets;
* completion count `5`;
* real outcomes projected through the existing field mission;
* fictional six-door count never used as business truth.

The current Colosseum component already enters field mode, tells the player to visit the real properties and record what actually happened, and withholds the final villain reveal until the real trace is complete.

This campaign is precedent.

Future campaigns should extend its philosophy, not build parallel systems.
