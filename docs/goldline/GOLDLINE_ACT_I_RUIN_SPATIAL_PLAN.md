# ACT I RUIN — SPATIAL PLAN
PROPOSED. One place. No lore dump. No extra toys.

You are always on the same parapet.
Everything Act I needs is visible from that parapet if you lean.

---

## TOP-DOWN (mental map)

```
                    HORIZON / SEA
                         |
              far hill · haze · heat
                         |
         [ colonnade ]------[ cut stone slope ]
              \                    /
               \     GOLD SEAM    /
                \   (Monday wire)/
                 \              /
                  \  PLUMB     /    <-- M03 parallel, slightly nearer, slack
                   \          /
                    PARAPET  ← YOU
                   /          \
                  /            \
           STRONGHOLD           open drop
           (left in default)    (right)
           wall + SHUTTER
           interior behind wall
           bench just inside
           the same doorway
```

North is sea. You face north-ish. Stronghold is a block on your **west**. Colonnade is **east-northeast**, hidden until lean-right. Seams run roughly west-east across the slope in front of you, closer than the hill, farther than the parapet lip.

You do not walk a level. You lean. That is the locomotion.

---

## DEFAULT PORTRAIT FRAME

**Foreground:** parapet lip, your side of the gold inlay starting at lower-left and running up-right across mid-stone. If M03 exists, the slack line sits a hand-width nearer, almost on the lip.

**Midground:** cut slope, the two seams, Stronghold wall entering from the left third. Shutter is a dark rectangle in that wall, readable but not centered. Bench is *suggested* as a slab in the doorway shadow — not a lab yet.

**Background:** sea, high horizon, far hill. Colonnade **not** in frame.

**Camera height:** standing adult at parapet. No drone.

Default is slightly “wrong”: seam off-center left, shutter cropped. First lean feels like fixing your stance.

---

## LEAN EXTREMES

**Lean left (toward Stronghold)**  
Shutter becomes the subject. You see wall thickness. Through a cracked edge, a hint of interior dark. After KEEP-night: warm spill from that same crack. Bench slab reads as furniture in the doorway, still the same doorway.

**Lean right (toward colonnade)**  
Colonnade enters from the right: three columns, then a fourth half-column. Monday gold, if it exists, can be framed so it *threads* a column gap — that is the postcard people will hunt. Sea widens.

**Lean does not:** go behind you, down the drop, or into a second map.

---

## LAYERS (what has to exist)

| Layer | Stuff |
|---|---|
| Near | parapet, slack plumb/bob, shutter face, doorway lip, bench when dollied |
| Mid | gold seam inlay, slope, Stronghold mass |
| Far | hill, colonnade, sea |

Gold must live in **mid**, nearer than hill, so Lean shears it. Slack line lives **nearer than gold** so the two never read as one stroke.

---

## CAMERA POSITIONS WE ACTUALLY NEED

Four. Not a rig.

1. **Overlook** — default. Lean lives here.
2. **Shutter close** — still Overlook, hard lean-left + slight settle. Same shot family.
3. **Wire close** — Overlook, slight settle down onto the touched seam. No cut.
4. **Bench push-in** — dolly forward along the doorway axis two meters. Stronghold interior side of the *same* wall. Sea still visible through the shutter opening if it is open, or as a slit if shut. This is M02 surgery. Dolly back on exit. Never a load screen.

M03 “hallway” is not a fifth map. It is Overlook after a small lateral settle **along the gold**, so Monday’s wire is seen from a few feet toward the colonnade — familiar ground, slightly wrong angle.

---

## SPATIAL MEMORY (what stays in more than one shot)

- The gold stroke’s *shape* — a kink near the Stronghold corner. Recognizable from Overlook, Wire close, and M03 settle.
- Shutter as a dark/warm rectangle in the left mass — visible in Overlook, dominant in lean-left, slit in Bench push-in.
- One colonnade column that appears at lean-right and is still *hinted* at the right edge of Overlook after you have been there once (we can cheat the default a pixel after first lean; optional).
- Parapet lip always under your hands.

If those four persist, it is one place.

---

## HOW M02 BECOMES SURGERY WITHOUT TELEPORT

The bench was always in the doorway.

When M02 is live, camera **dollies** from Overlook through that doorway. Light goes harder, narrower. Slope and sea remain in the shutter slit behind you so you never left.

Thumb work happens on the slab. Lid seats. Dolly back. You are on the parapet again. Same kink in the gold.

Forbidden: a white room, a lab UI, a different sky.

---

## HOW M03 FEELS LIKE RETURNING

Armed: slack line appears **on the parapet side** of Monday gold — same stone, nearer.

Play: camera settles along the gold toward the colonnade (the path your body already walked in the fiction). You see Monday’s kink from the side. The bob hangs off a tick cut into that same stone.

You are not in a new corridor. You are standing slightly to the right of where you stood Monday.

---

## HOW THE SHUTTER STAYS THE SAME STRONGHOLD

It is the only opening in the west wall. Every frame that includes the Stronghold includes that rectangle, even tiny.

Pending Hold: sun-stain climbs **that** exterior face.
KEEP: spill from **that** interior.
MISS: **that** leaf rattles.

If we ever show a second window, we have broken the toy.

---

## CONTINUITY WALKTHROUGH

**Monday after M01**  
Overlook. Dead seam is now taut gold with the kink. Slack line absent. Shutter dark, stuck if tried. Lean-right: colonnade, gold threading a gap if you hunt. Night has not occupied.

**Wednesday, M03 armed**  
Same Overlook. Gold unchanged. Slack line and bob now on the near lip. Lean-left still the same shutter. Lean-right still the same three columns. The place gained a piece of metal, not a new level.

**Thursday, M04 pending**  
Same. Light climbing the Stronghold face you already knew. Gold and bob still there if M03 isn’t resolved — weather plus a clock on the same wall.

**Thursday night, KEEP**  
Same camera family. Spill from the shutter cracks. Lift the same leaf. Sea through the thickness. Lean-right colonnade now against night water. Gold still mid-slope. You could draw the wire in the dark; it would still be Monday.

**Monday after MISS instead**  
Same night or next morning: no spill. Same leaf, rattle. Gold from M01 still mid-slope. If M03 existed it is still slack. The ruin did not reset. Only the window failed.

Five dates. One kink. One shutter. One colonnade. That is the proof.

---

## MINIMUM GEOMETRY

Build this and nothing else for the 15-minute box:

- Parapet slab + lip
- Slope plane with **two groove paths** (gold / slack)
- One kink in the gold groove
- Stronghold box with **one** shutter leaf
- Doorway + bench slab (two meters of interior, not a house)
- Three columns + half column
- Hill card + sea card
- A hanging bob + tick mark
- Sun-stain that can climb the Stronghold face
- Night spill plane behind the shutter

No second building. No beach walk. No interior maze. No extra apertures.

If that set cannot make thirty seconds of lean feel like a place, adding rooms will not help.
