# Lantern City — Beverly Hills (Commercial Row)

Silver Lake is warm outreach: neighbours in ordinary apartments who used to order and went quiet for 60+ days.
Beverly Hills is the other half of the job, **cold outreach to commercial prospects**, so it looks and plays
differently: a sunlit luxury plaza with physically based lighting, a velvet rope, and a doorman who is
unimpressed by laundry.

Entry: Lantern City board → click the Beverly Hills island (explore mode), or
`/growth/lantern-city?level=beverly-hills`. **Return to City** goes back to the board.

## The gag (the velvet rope)

Sterling, the doorman at the hotel, turns the heroine away ("Deliveries use the service entrance." / "Beverly
Hills doesn't have one."). Only *somebodies* get in, so she has to become one:

1. **A $26 green juice.** The juice bar queue is three influencers filming content. She gets in line by
   photobombing each of their shots until they give up and leave.
2. **A handbag dog.** Biscotti the pug got loose. Catch him with a sprint (he runs faster when you're close) and he
   rides in her backpack.
3. **Sunglasses worth more than your rent.** They're on the golden poodle statue on top of the fountain. Hop up the
   tiers.

With all three, Sterling lifts the rope: paparazzi flashes and confetti, and Claire tells you who is actually behind
it. **Skip the rope** is always there for a busy operator.

State machine: `client/src/pages/goldline/beverlyHills3d/velvetRope.ts` (pure, unit tested).

## Truth rules

- Prospects are **real records only**: `server/experience/lanternCity/beverlyHillsProspects.ts` reads the active
  tenant's commercial accounts in Beverly Hills / 9021x, plus the Ten Doors (`DAY1_TARGETS`) Beverly Hills entries
  for the default tenant. Nothing is invented. If the read fails, the desk says so (`readable: false`), which is
  not the same as having no prospects.
- Drafts (`shared/beverlyHillsProspects.ts → prospectDraft`) state facts only. Follow-up wording ("good to meet
  you") appears only when the record shows an in-person conversation.
- **The level never sends anything.** Email opens the operator's own mail app (`mailto:`), Call opens the dialer
  (`tel:`), Directions opens maps; status reads "Prepared, not sent. You send it yourself." The e2e spec asserts
  no tRPC mutation is posted during the whole level.

## Rendering

three.js PBR with ACES tone mapping, an HDR environment for lighting and a painted canvas sky, triplanar detail
textures by material-name prefix, swaying palm shader, fountain spray, string lights, scattering pigeons. Post:
GTAO (high-quality devices only) → bloom → grade → output. Measured on an M1 with Metal: ~34 fps at 1600×900 with
GTAO, 60 fps at phone size.

## Asset provenance (all CC0)

- Quaternius Universal Base Characters + Universal Animation Library 1 & 2 (characters and clips).
- Kenney Car Kit 3.1 (parked cars).
- Poly Haven textures (detail maps).
- Everything else (plaza, props, pug, signs, sky) is generated by the scripts above.

## Known limits

- Characters are stylised base meshes with modelled garments, not the concept-art fidelity of a paid pack.
- Software WebGL (CI) runs it slowly but correctly; a device without WebGL gets an honest "closed for a private
  event" screen, and the HUD (Skip, desk) still works.
