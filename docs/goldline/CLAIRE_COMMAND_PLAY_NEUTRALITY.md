# Claire, Goldline Command / Play, and surface neutrality

There is one Claire.

Goldline Command and Goldline Play are experience editions over the same
operator/business brain. Individual / Team / Enterprise are commercial plan
tiers, not experience names, and they must not change relationship tier,
canon access, or trust dimensions.

Mobile Driver and desktop Admin reach Claire through different surfaces.
They share:

- operator identity (`tenantId` + `operatorUserId`)
- macro goals
- work history
- field outcomes
- relevant memory
- action / permission state
- relationship state
- MissionSalesBrief

They do not have:

- a mobile Claire brain
- a desktop Sage brain
- a game-created source of business truth

`assembleClaireDriveContext` is the shared read path. Surface labels may
appear in telemetry only.
