# GOLDLINE — REAL-WORLD FIELD PROOF (Slice 30)

The only steps here are ones software cannot perform. Everything verifiable
without a human is already covered by automated gates; nothing below asks you
to re-check something a test could have.

**Before you leave:** open Goldline Admin and note the current state of one
building you plan to visit — its lantern colour and any scars. That is your
"before" reading.

1. **Start Goldline on the phone.** Open the Driver app. Do not build a plan;
   the day should already exist. *Record: did the world open, or did a task
   list?*
2. **Take the objective the game gives you.** Note which building and why it
   claims you are being sent. Tap "Why is this here?" if present.
   *Record: was the stated reason true?*
3. **Drive there.** At some point the app should conclude you are driving and
   stop asking for attention. *Record: did it, and roughly when?*
4. **Park and walk to the door.** Wait for arrival to register — up to ~12
   seconds standing still within ~55m. *Record: how long, and did it fire
   before you actually arrived?*
5. **Confirm arrival did NOT complete anything.** Progress should still read
   as unvisited. *Record: any button offering to mark it done without you
   speaking to someone is a truth-firewall defect — screenshot it.*
6. **Do the real thing.** Speak to whoever is there, or discover nobody is.
7. **Record what actually happened**, including the unflattering options:
   decision maker unavailable · spoke, no decision · follow-up agreed · won ·
   lost. *Record: were you ever forced to overstate the outcome?*
8. **If a date was mentioned**, say it into the field journal the way you would
   to a person ("she said try Thursday"). *Record: did it become an
   appointment when it was only a suggestion?*
9. **Open Admin on a laptop, same account.** Find the same building.
   *Record: is it the SAME building — one entry, not a duplicate? Did the city
   change versus your "before" reading?*
10. **Next morning, open Driver again.** *Record: did yesterday's unresolved
    thing come back on its own, on the right day, without you re-entering it?*

**If anything breaks the illusion** — a moment where you felt you stopped
playing and started using software — note where. That list is worth more than
any bug report.

**Screenshot on failure:** the screen, plus Admin's view of the same building.

## What this proves that software could not
Real GPS under real conditions; a real conversation; that the same physical
place is one identity across two surfaces on two devices; and that tomorrow
remembers without manual entry.
