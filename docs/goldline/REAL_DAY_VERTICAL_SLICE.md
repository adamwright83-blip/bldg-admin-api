# JOYSTICK Real-Day Vertical Slice

Status: active implementation plan.

This sequence exists to force the current JOYSTICK stack through one complete real business day. It is not a new planner, world, taxonomy, Claire brain, or Kingdom expansion.

## Non-negotiable constraint

Do not add:

- a second planner;
- a second work graph beside Day Line;
- a new world or Kingdom;
- a new Objective/Mission taxonomy;
- broad Claire cognition architecture;
- speculative business truth.

Reuse the existing authoritative systems and make one real Objective survive every surface.

## Slice 1 — True Book

Goal: no product surface may treat ambiguous or stale economic state as authoritative.

Required:

1. Native paid customer/order truth requires real processor evidence, not a boolean flag alone.
2. CleanCloud freshness comes from the canonical source-coverage contract.
3. Stale/partial/unavailable CleanCloud remains readable but must never be described as exhaustive/current/zero.
4. Paid-book readers, Claire, progression, churn, Day Line ranking, and world reactions must consume the same authority semantics rather than inventing local freshness rules.
5. “Booked”, “paid”, “new paying customer”, and equivalent economic outcomes must not be asserted from a stripped/held/unverified card state.

Acceptance:

- deterministic tests prove unverified native paid flags do not mint paying-customer progression;
- stale CleanCloud never licenses exhaustive/current claims;
- exact revenue remains gated by the canonical economic-event span and payment evidence;
- no model decides freshness.

## Slice 2 — Parking-Lot Clerk

Goal: a real field stop persists what happened, not merely that the operator was nearby.

One durable post-stop observation contract:

- attached to the same Objective/visit identity;
- timestamped;
- operator-reported, never mislabeled as independently verified;
- one concise Claire prompt: “What did they actually say?”;
- preserves freeform operator wording plus structured outcome only when deterministically derivable or explicitly confirmed;
- survives app restart and is readable by Admin, Claire, Day Line, and Narrator OS.

GPS proves presence. The clerk records operator testimony. Neither substitutes for the other.

## Slice 3 — One Stop Is the Map

Goal: one real building is one object through the whole product.

The same Objective identity must be:

1. selected/ranked by Day Line;
2. rendered as a node in the existing Overworld;
3. made playable by the existing arrival/verification rules;
4. completed with the real-world visit;
5. debriefed through the Parking-Lot Clerk;
6. reflected in authoritative business state;
7. eligible for exactly one authored world consequence.

No duplicate “game mission” ID for the same Tuesday work.

## Slice 4 — Real-Day Acceptance

This is an exam, not a feature epic.

On a real phone:

1. leave the house;
2. open Driver;
3. complete one pickup;
4. complete one real sales stop;
5. send one real follow-up/dormant-customer text;
6. kill the browser/app;
7. reopen;
8. ask Claire what happened;
9. compare Claire, Admin, Day Line, and world state.

Acceptance requires identity and outcome agreement across all surfaces. Any disagreement becomes the next ticket.

## After the vertical slice holds

Then proceed to:

1. weekly readiness / Monday morning;
2. unresolved-work Echoes;
3. one consolidated Rook pass: Clockhead evidence → Coastal hunt → durable Rook → travel together → CONTACT demonstrated through authored behavior.

Do not expand Kingdom Two before the real-day acceptance passes.
