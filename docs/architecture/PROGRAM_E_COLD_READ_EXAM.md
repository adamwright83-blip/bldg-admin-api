# Program E8 — independent cold-model comprehension exam

**Status: prepared, not executed.** Use on an unfamiliar model with no user chat memory, prior assistant summaries, or unpublished architecture prompt. Give it repository checkout and `ARCHITECTURE.md` only as an entrypoint; let it inspect sources.

## Questions

1. What is JOYSTICK, what is Goldline, and which elements operate on reality versus fiction?
2. Who are Claire, Daphne, President and Mitch? Can President delegate to Mitch?
3. Who legally creates, transitions and deletes a native Order? Is Driver a Vendor?
4. Which evidence admits a native Payment occurrence? Can order status/price establish captured dollars?
5. What exactly means `commercial won`? Is it payment?
6. Is a CleanCloud order native JOYSTICK payment truth? What happens to tenantless historical evidence?
7. Where does Mission Director rank, and where does Day Line commit?
8. Does a game event, browser event, GPS visit or narrative claim establish real customer conversion?
9. Where do you change a Driver delivery rule? Which tests enforce the authority boundary?
10. Where do you change Claire's persistent communication preference? Identify current protected PRs.
11. Identify the canonical live Lantern City renderer and distinguish `businessWorld`, `driverGameWorld`, `goldlineWorld` and `worldForge`.
12. Identify live legacy compatibility code and explain why it may not be removed blindly.
13. Provide three claims verified from source lines, three inferences, and every materially uninspected area as UNKNOWN.

## Passing rubric

Require evidence for each material answer (actual path/function/line, not just this overview). Mark failure for any invented payment admission, implicit default tenant, conflation of commercial win with payment, false claim that game writes reality, conflation of President and Mitch, or unverified legacy deletion. Mark UNKNOWN where model has insufficient access. Independently repeat with multiple cold models. Do not self-certify from the existence of the guide or from the authoring model's own answers.
